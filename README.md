# Peetopia
A physics-based game where you help Bob overcome his daily bathroom struggles.

## Story
The year was 20XX. There was a man named Bob.
He lived a normal life, but he always had one major problem: the toilet moves to a different location everyday.
One day, it might be clipping through the floor. Another day, it might be floating in the air.7

## How to Play
1. **Aim:** Adjust the direction of his thing to change the launch angle.
2. **Adjust the Intensity:** Set the initial velocity of the urine.
3. **Launch:** Release and watch the trajectory of his urine!
